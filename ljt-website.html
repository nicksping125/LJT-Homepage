About

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in:

  • LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
  • Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
  • LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
